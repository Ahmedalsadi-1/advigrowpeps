Research peptides in the United States are marketed exclusively as research chemicals under the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act. Marketing, labeling, or selling these compounds for human therapeutic use is unlawful without FDA approval. Advigrow Peptides sells only to verified research end-users, reserves the right to refuse any order, and prohibits resale for non-research purposes. Orders are accepted from qualified research buyers only. Prices are listed per vial in USD. Purchaser confirms research end-user status at checkout. Advigrow Peptides reserves the right to cancel orders that fall outside stated research-use terms, with a full refund issued before shipment.

Shipping

Orders ship from U.S. facilities. In-stock items typically ship within 1–2 business days. Lyophilized peptides are packaged to protect compound integrity during transit. Storage guidance: −20 °C lyophilized; 2–8 °C after reconstitution; protect from light and moisture; avoid freeze–thaw cycles.

Returns & Support

Order cancellations for a full refund are available before shipment.
