01 /The Process

Four gates between synthesis and your bench.

01

Synthesis

Solid-phase peptide synthesis in US facilities, with fatty-acid conjugation performed at the same stage for batch consistency.

02

Purification

Multi-stage reverse-phase HPLC drives each lot to ≥99% purity before analytical release.

03

Verification

Independent third-party labs confirm identity by mass spectrometry, screen heavy metals and endotoxins, and verify net peptide content.

04

Documentation

Batch-matched COAs — chromatograms, MS profiles, and purity results — are published with the lot, before purchase.
